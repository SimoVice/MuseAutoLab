## What the recording establishes

The researcher describes equipment and constraints, compares two sourced alternatives,
chooses Jubilee, lowers its digital bed, requests a cold-bay concept, saves/reopens and
adds an inspection-arm/camera concept. Late quantities produce deterministic preparation
calculations. The confirmed project generates real CAD, provenance and guide files.

All main shots belong to the same project: revision 9 at completion, 120 mm bed drop,
8 °C requested target and 200/100/20 µL stock volumes. The earlier example in
`docs/examples/demo-project.json` is a different recorded project and is not spliced
into this film. The incompatible 450 mm request was recorded after the successful
journey and is explicitly shown out of recording order.

Waits are shortened and identified. The actual CAD generation took **137.757 s**;
the uncut source retains that wait. Reopened-result footage states that it makes no
new inference. The live conversation was sampled from actual browser screenshots at
up to 4 fps with measured intervals; the separate export capture is native 25 fps.
The finished composition is 30 fps. Original recordings and the editing project remain
local; the [curated live record](../examples/delivery-third-rehearsal.json) and
[verification report](../verification.md) preserve the relevant outcomes and limits.
